National Outreach

Serving Underprivileged Regions Across India

While based in Baramulla (J&K), IPRO is legally mandated by its Trust Deed to expand humanitarian operations, social rehabilitation, and animal welfare across the nation.

JK

Jammu & Kashmir

Headquarters at Baramulla & Pattan. Programs in youth de-addiction, winter relief, free clinics, and animal protection.

UP

Uttar Pradesh

Outreach focusing on artisan vocational skills, orphan schooling, and emergency veterinary clinics.

WB

West Bengal

Disaster flood aid, rural drinking water plants, child education initiatives, and livestock emergency shelter.

BR

Bihar

Disaster relief food rations, school infrastructure support, and rural community medical camps.

AS

Assam

Flood rescue assistance, women micro-enterprise training, and wildlife preservation awareness.

DL

Delhi NCR

Urban slum outreach, child education assistance, legal aid support, and stray animal rescue networks.
