Regional Outreach

Serving High-Density Muslim Regions Across India

While based in Baramulla (J&K), IPRO is legally mandated by its Trust Deed to expand humanitarian operations and relief networks across the nation.

JK

Jammu & Kashmir

Headquarters at Baramulla & Pattan. Ground programs in de-addiction, orphan welfare, winter relief, and healthcare camps.

UP

Uttar Pradesh

Active networks in Moradabad, Bareilly, Aligarh, and Lucknow focusing on artisan skill training and higher education access.

WB

West Bengal

Support programs in Murshidabad, Malda, and North 24 Parganas for rural drinking water, flood aid, and child schooling.

BR

Bihar (Seemanchal)

Serving Kishanganj, Araria, and Katihar with disaster emergency food packets, madrasa modernization, and healthcare.

AS

Assam

Field aid across Dhubri, Barpeta, and Nagaon for flood relief, legal documentation assistance, and women tailoring units.

KL

Kerala

Partnerships in Malappuram and Kozhikode for higher education counseling and specialized medical care coordination.

TG

Telangana (Hyderabad)

Urban slum outreach in Old City Hyderabad for destitute widow monthly rations, youth job skills, and dialysis support.

DL

Delhi NCR

Assisting migrant families in Okhla, Seelampur, and Mewat borders with legal aid, child education, and winter blankets.

MH

Maharashtra

Vocational and medical assistance in Govandi, Bhiwandi, Malegaon, and Aurangabad for informal sector labor households.
